# Common service requests

The Spot agent supports the same core insurance workflows across web chat,
agent email, iMessage, and Slack. The presentation differs by channel: web chat
shows the richest evidence and action controls, while text and Slack keep the
reply concise and preserve the work in a Spot thread.

## Policy questions and comparisons

Include the policy number, named insured, carrier, or line of business when more
than one policy could match. Ask for source evidence when exact wording matters.

Examples:

- `What is the business interruption waiting period? Cite the policy wording.`
- `Compare the expiring and renewal cyber limits and exclusions.`
- `Which policy covers hired and non-owned autos?`

A preliminary policy can support a broad summary. Exact evidence, confirmation,
and consequential workflows wait for final processing.

## Certificates of insurance

Provide:

- the policy or insured entity;
- the certificate holder's legal name and full address;
- the purpose or project;
- any additional-insured, waiver, primary/noncontributory, or wording request;
- the delivery recipient; and
- the deadline.

Spot uses the finalized source-backed policy facts and may reuse a current
certificate for the same holder and policy version. It holds the request if the
holder is ambiguous, the requested evidence is unavailable, or the policy is
not final. Review the generated certificate before sending it to an outside
party.

## Policy changes and endorsements

State the exact change, requested effective date, affected entity or location,
and any supporting documentation. Spot may ask follow-up questions, create a
review item, or prepare an email rather than treating the conversation itself
as a bound change.

Examples:

- `Add the new Austin location effective September 1. What information is missing?`
- `Request removal of vehicle 23 from the commercial auto policy.`
- `Show the status of the endorsement request in this thread.`

A Spot message is not carrier confirmation that coverage has changed. Rely on
the issued endorsement or other formal evidence from the provider or carrier.

## Compliance checks

Spot can compare the client's own policies with insurance requirements and can
review connected-vendor policies requirement by requirement. Identify whether
you mean **our insurance** or a **vendor's insurance**, and name the requirement
set or relationship.

Examples:

- `Why is our landlord requirement marked noncompliant?`
- `Which vendors have general liability expiring in the next 30 days?`
- `Show the exact limits that fail this vendor requirement.`

Treat a compliance result as a source-backed workflow aid, not legal advice.
Resolve ambiguous wording with the provider or requirement owner.

## Mailbox search and document intake

Name the mailbox, sender, approximate date, subject, and desired action. Search
results do not automatically become workspace records.

Examples:

- `Search my mailbox for the newest Chubb renewal email.`
- `Read result 2 and save its PDF to this thread.`
- `Import the policy attachment from that message.`
- `Save the landlord's insurance requirements as a requirement record.`

Connected-mailbox scope controls who can perform the live search. Once imported,
the selected policy, requirement, attachment, or company fact becomes
organization-visible workspace data.

## Email drafting and sending

Tell Spot the recipients, goal, facts that must be included, attachments, and
tone. Ask for a draft first when the message changes coverage, represents an
official position, or goes outside the organization.

Examples:

- `Draft a follow-up to the provider asking for the missing endorsement.`
- `Prepare an email to the certificate holder with the COI attached. Do not send.`
- `Revise draft 1 to include the project number, then show it again.`

Check **To**, **Cc**, content, and attachments before confirming a send. In
iMessage, use `/drafts`, `/send 1`, and `/discard 1` to manage drafts associated
with that conversation.

## Company facts and notes

Spot can save stable company context when authorized. State that you want the
fact saved and include its source. Do not save temporary instructions, guesses,
credentials, or another organization's confidential information as durable
company context.

## Choose the right channel

- Use **web chat** for exact evidence, several files, comparisons, and review
  controls.
- Use **agent email** when the request belongs in an existing email chain or
  includes approved outside participants.
- Use **iMessage** for concise mobile requests, voice notes, and direct status
  checks.
- Use **Slack** for team-visible collaboration and a human handoff in the
  dedicated support channel.

For any request, an explicit confirmation does not override workspace access,
document-readiness checks, or delivery safeguards.
