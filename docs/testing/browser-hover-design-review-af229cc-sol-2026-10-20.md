### The six invariants remain mandatory

| Hover requirement (P525–541) | Evidence supplied in af229cc | Why other checks do not replace it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same written instruction/checkbox label | Original whole frame vs post-motion whole frame | Same URL, generation and frame owner can retain a newly rendered instruction. |
| Same pictures in same positions | Same whole-frame pair | A canvas/CSS picture can change without a structural/text DOM event. “Still matches the requested object” is weaker than same picture. |

## Post-hover fresh revalidation alternative

Consider a single bounded state machine: keep the original goal, skill plan, frame owner/document/generation, target/Rect/point, and before pixels; perform the required curve; capture fresh; revalidate; capture exactly the approved state again; perform final hit/fences; press once. Whether this preserves the contract depends on what “revalidate” proves:

| Variant | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Discard the old before/plan; locate a currently valid checkbox/tile, rerun presence/action relevance and click it | **Reject. Violates changed-content refusal.** New validity does not prove continuity with old authorization. |
| Retain original target coordinates/owner, but merely ask whether it is currently unsolved/actionable/matches the task | **Reject.** A replaced tile can still match the same instruction, a changed instruction can still permit a click, or a selected state can be represented by color. Same point/frame is insufficient. |
| Keep original before/intent/ownership and require all six unchanged semantic properties at `.9`, original containment and all exact recapture/document/geometry/hit fences; optionally recheck current action policy | **Contract-preserving but not a fix.** It is substantially the existing native continuity design, with possibly another policy check. The two refusals remain decisive; additional revalidation cannot override them. |
| Treat refreshed content as a new plan after a native refusal, automatically reset baseline, and try again | **Reject as an implicit retry/authorization reset.** It circumvents the old continuity refusal. Legitimate skill replanning remains bounded at its explicit round boundary; no extra click or baseline laundering inside one press is justified. |

An additive fresh check can reduce stale observation risk but must retain the original binding and reject any six-invariant failure. A newly refined point cannot silently become the original target: it would need original control identity/shape/position proof, appropriate movement and another final capture. Continuing on a new target, widening the region, or sending a second curve after final approval loses the approved state/point binding. No new press may be authorized from a command ACK or an unknown earlier outcome.

A separate trusted renderer-specific proof of exactly cosmetic change could in principle replace some probabilistic judgments **only through an explicit manager-reviewed architecture change**. Generic untrusted DOM/class/style declarations, pixel containment, a border mask inferred from the same Box, or a screenshot of a valid current target do not supply that proof. CSS-only selection can be visually indistinguishable from an innocuous color hover without baseline semantics. The current evidence provides no such trusted semantic channel for arbitrary provider iframes. I therefore reject a general fresh-only replacement under the existing requirements, rather than claim it is feasible because one demo could pass.
