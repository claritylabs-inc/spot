# Provider contact research — October 22, 2026

All 82 external providers in the local development directory received at least one
publicly published contact method: 159 contacts, including 66 people with titles.
Each stored contact retains its source URL, evidence and research timestamp.
Names and roles come from associated official team/contact blocks; email patterns
were not guessed. Coverage preferences remain empty unless a source explicitly
routes that line to the contact. These public details have not been confirmed by
email or telephone.

The data was saved through authenticated, audited `brokerContacts.save` calls to
the local Convex instance at port 3220. Production and provider memberships were
not changed. Raw pages and reviewed/import artifacts are under the ignored local
`.context/provider-contacts/` directory. This document is an audit summary, not a
production data migration.
