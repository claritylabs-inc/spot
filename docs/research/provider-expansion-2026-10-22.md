## Remaining placement and verification gaps

This is a broad researched directory, not a guarantee that any request can be insured. Industry, limits, forms, capacity, appointment and individual producer authority still require placement review. Finland has an evidenced industrial carrier channel but insufficient verified SME broker depth; several smaller European markets also have fewer alternatives. Tryg and Fennia were not added using privacy/media addresses in place of business contacts.

Canadian statutory workers compensation and public auto programs need their appropriate public routes. US monopoly workers-compensation jurisdictions cannot be treated as private placements from WORK tags alone. RIBO and several registry lookups were blocked or incomplete; unknown results do not establish unlicensed activity. Nationwide marketing is retained as service-area evidence where supported, with partial licensing verification explicitly separated.
