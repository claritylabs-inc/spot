## Evidence boundaries

- The audit covers named records and explicit jurisdictions, not every licence for every existing directory provider.
- A national brand, federal registration, foreign parent, home licence, appointment or office address does not establish all local authorizations.
- Agency licences, representatives, MGAs, insurance writers, reinsurers and surplus-lines eligibility are kept distinct.
- Country-level ACORD appetite cannot express all line-specific territorial restrictions. Known conflicts are narrowed conservatively.
- No provider was contacted, appointed, bound or asked to quote. Published email and domain MX do not verify mailbox delivery.

Regulator evidence includes Saskatchewan, Alberta, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, British Columbia, Quebec AMF, Texas TDI, dated California LASLI lists, FCA, EEA home/host registers and Nordic authorities. Exact entities and country scope control; a Texas agency mailing address is not an authority grant in that address's state. A representative licence is not a corporate agency licence. Carrier admission, surplus-lines eligibility and reinsurance status remain distinct.

Notable reconciliations include active successor/MGA-class Saskatchewan records, Corgi agency versus carrier authority, Kinsale insurer versus historical reinsurer status, Aqueous's current Ryan Specialty entity, Arachas's UK runoff restriction and current FCA commercial permissions for PolicyBee/James Hallam/Adler Fairways.
